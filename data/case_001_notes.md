# Case 001 — Fake Job Recruitment Investigation

## 1. Target

- Domain: fpci.org.in
- Claimed organization: Food Processing Corporation of India
- Investigation type: Fake recruitment / job posting OSINT
- Case period: 2023

---

## 2. Job Posting Information

The website fpci.org.in advertised recruitment opportunities using the
name "Food Processing Corporation of India."

According to the Food Corporation of India public notice, the fake
website displayed an "Apply For Job" section and showed:

- Application deadline: 26.05.2023
- Recruitment/job application through the website
- The website presented itself as "Food Processing Corporation Of India"

Source:
Food Corporation of India — Public Notice: Recruitment Fraud

URL:
https://fci.gov.in/assets/public-notice/fpci3.pdf

---

## 3. Government Verification

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries issued a caution notice
dated 16 May 2023 regarding the website fpci.org.in.

The Ministry stated that:

- It did not operate the website fpci.org.in.
- It had not advertised the posts promoted through the website.
- The fake website used the name "Food Processing Corporation of India."
- Job aspirants were asked to submit applications and make payments.
- Official Ministry recruitment is conducted through authorized
  recruitment channels such as SSC and UPSC.

Source:
Ministry of Food Processing Industries — Caution Notice

URL:
https://www.mofpi.gov.in/sites/default/files/fakeWebsite.pdf

---

## 4. FCI Verification

The Food Corporation of India also issued a public notice about
recruitment fraud.

The notice states that fpci.org.in was a fake recruitment website
using the name "Food Processing Corporation of India."

FCI stated that the advertisement on fpci.org.in had no relation to
its recruitment.

FCI also stated that its recruitment information is provided through
its official website:

https://fci.gov.in

Source:
Food Corporation of India — Public Notice: Recruitment Fraud

URL:
https://fci.gov.in/assets/public-notice/fpci3.pdf

---

## 5. Initial Red Flags

### Red Flag 1 — Unauthorized recruitment website

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries stated that it did not
operate fpci.org.in.

### Red Flag 2 — False organizational association

The website used the name "Food Processing Corporation of India" while
official organizations stated that the recruitment was not authorized.

### Red Flag 3 — Payment request

The Ministry stated that job aspirants were asked to submit applications
and make payment.

### Red Flag 4 — Recruitment advertisement not authorized

The Ministry stated that it had not advertised the posts promoted
through the fake website.

### Red Flag 5 — Fake recruitment presentation

The FCI notice contains a screenshot of the website showing an
"Apply For Job" section and a recruitment deadline.

---

## 6. Initial Assessment

The available government notices provide strong evidence that
fpci.org.in was an unauthorized recruitment website.

The findings should be treated as OSINT indicators supported by
official sources. The investigation does not independently establish
criminal liability.
