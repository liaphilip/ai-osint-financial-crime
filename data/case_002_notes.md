# Case 002 — NRDRM Fake Recruitment Investigation

## 1. Target

- Claimed organization: National Rural Development & Recreation Mission (NRDRM)
- Domains identified by Ministry of Rural Development:
  - nrdrm.com
  - nrdrmvacancy.com
- Investigation type: Fake recruitment / Government impersonation OSINT
- Case period: 2025

---

## 2. Government Warning

The Ministry of Rural Development issued a public warning on
17 February 2025 regarding fraudulent recruitment activities conducted
by an organization calling itself National Rural Development &
Recreation Mission (NRDRM).

The Ministry stated that NRDRM did not work under the purview of the
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, as claimed.

The notice identified the following websites:

- www.nrdrm.com
- www.nrdrmvacancy.com

The Ministry warned that recruitment activities conducted by NRDRM
purportedly in the name of the Ministry or its officials could be
considered fraudulent and had no government endorsement.

The Ministry also stated that it does not charge any fee at any stage
of its recruitment process and does not request applicants' bank
account information.

Source:
Press Information Bureau — Ministry of Rural Development

Date:
17.02.2025

## 3. Government Action

On 4 April 2025, the Department of Rural Development reported further
action regarding the fake NRDRM recruitment operation.

The Department stated that it had identified a fake NRDRM website
impersonating Department content and calling applications for various
posts.

The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) was requested to
take down the fraudulent NRDRM websites.

The Department stated that:

- I4C removed the related NRDRM websites from the Internet.
- A disclaimer was published on the Ministry website.
- The public was cautioned about the fraudulent websites and fake
  recruitment drive.
- The Department lodged an FIR regarding the matter.

Source:
Press Information Bureau — Ministry of Rural Development

## 5. Initial Red Flags

### Red Flag 1 — False government association

NRDRM claimed to operate under the Ministry of Rural Development,
but the Ministry explicitly stated that it did not.

### Red Flag 2 — Fake recruitment activity

The Ministry warned that recruitment activities conducted by NRDRM
in the name of the Ministry or its officials could be fraudulent.

### Red Flag 3 — Unauthorized websites

The Ministry identified nrdrm.com and nrdrmvacancy.com as websites
associated with the fraudulent recruitment operation.

### Red Flag 4 — Government impersonation

The Department later stated that the NRDRM website was impersonating
Department content.

### Red Flag 5 — Recruitment fee warning

The Ministry stated that it does not charge any fee at any stage of
its recruitment process.

### Red Flag 6 — Government enforcement action

I4C was requested to take down the fraudulent websites, and the
Department stated that an FIR was lodged.

---

## 6. Initial Assessment

Official Government of India sources provide strong evidence that the
NRDRM recruitment operation was unauthorized and falsely associated
with the Ministry of Rural Development.

The investigation identifies OSINT indicators based on official public
sources and does not independently establish criminal liability.
